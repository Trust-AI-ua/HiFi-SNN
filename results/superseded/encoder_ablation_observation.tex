% Observation paragraph for the encoder-ablation results (Table \ref{tab:encoder_ablation}).
% No em dashes. Numbers are means over 5 seeds from the completed 5-arm sweep.
The ablation in Table~\ref{tab:encoder_ablation} decomposes the teacher's
contribution into its two possible channels: the placement of the encoding
thresholds (its ruler) and the soft labels used for distillation (its
posterior). The ruler is the load-bearing component. The \texttt{quantile}
arm, which keeps channels, pruning, budget, and per-feature edge counts
identical to \texttt{tree} and changes only the edge placement from XGBoost
split points to equal-frequency quantiles, degrades macro-F1 on 9 of the 11
datasets, by up to $10.8$ points (CICIoT2023: $72.2 \rightarrow 61.4$;
KDDCup99: $61.8 \rightarrow 52.7$; NSL-KDD: $58.5 \rightarrow 50.8$), and
simultaneously raises the SOP count by $8$--$79\%$ at the same channel width,
because poorly placed thresholds produce spike codes that drive more hidden
activity. The teacher's split points therefore carry class-boundary
information that a purely data-driven binning of the same resolution does not.
The posterior contributes a smaller but consistent gain: removing the
distillation terms (\texttt{no-KD}, $\alpha_{\mathrm{kd}} =
\alpha_{\mathrm{rank}} = 0$) lowers macro-F1 on 9 of 11 datasets, typically by
$0.3$--$1.9$ points and by $6.9$ points on the hardest, most imbalanced split
(CSE-CIC-IDS2018: $75.9 \rightarrow 69.0$). The remaining arms bound the
channel budget rather than the teacher: \texttt{uniform} performs comparably
to \texttt{tree}, indicating that importance-proportional weighting by itself
contributes little once the thresholds are tree-derived, while \texttt{all}
roughly doubles the SOPs for at most $+2.5$ macro-F1, so the pruned budget is
a favourable operating point on the channels-versus-accuracy curve rather
than a quality optimum. Finally, the distilled student tracks its GBDT
teacher's ceiling to within $1$--$2$ macro-F1 on most datasets (NSL-KDD:
$58.5$ versus $59.9$) while replacing the ensemble's dense inference with a
$\sim$9K-parameter spiking network, which is the premise of building the
student in the first place.
